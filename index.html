FAQ

Questions, answered

How do I book an appointment?

Call 0913 833 338, message us on Telegram (@Painkiller38), or fill in the booking form below and we'll confirm with you.

Are clear aligners (Invisalign) right for me?

Aligners suit many mild to moderate alignment concerns. After a short consultation we'll tell you honestly whether aligners or braces will give the best result.

Does a root canal hurt?

The procedure is done under local anesthesia, so it should feel similar to getting a filling. It's usually what relieves the pain of an infected tooth.

Is teeth whitening safe?

Professional whitening done after an examination is safe and effective. We'll check your teeth and gums first to make sure it's suitable for you.

Do you treat children?

Yes. We offer gentle family and children's dentistry in a calm, friendly environment.
